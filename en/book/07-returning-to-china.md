# 7. The Step of Returning to China

This section counts money and time. When you leave Japan for China, you need to close out your resident registration, residence card, taxes and pension on the Japanese side, and connect your credential evaluation, tax status and social insurance on the Chinese side. Most of these have deadlines, and they get much harder once you have left. So the order matters more than any single item.

Half of this section is new items. The other half strings together items from earlier sections in time order. The Japanese provisions come from the current text of the Basic Resident Register Act (住民基本台帳法), the Immigration Control Act (入管法), the Income Tax Act (所得税法), the Local Tax Act (地方税法), the Act on General Rules for National Taxes (国税通則法) and the National Pension Act (国民年金法). The credential evaluation part uses the guide of the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (教育部留学服务中心, CSCSE). All as of 2026-10-05.

Start with this checklist in time order. The details are in the items below and in earlier sections.

**After deciding to return, before you leave**

- Work out your pension once: how many years you paid in Japan, and whether to take the lump-sum withdrawal payment (脱退一時金) or keep the years toward the 10. See Section 3, Item 3 (the 10-year line for receiving a pension) and Section 3, Item 4 (applying for the lump-sum withdrawal payment after leaving Japan).
- Leaving your job: settle your pay and ask for a certificate of leaving employment. See Section 2, Item 10 (two things to ask for when you leave a job).
- Moving out of your home: give notice ahead as the contract requires, and take photos on the day you move out. See Section 5, Item 8 (for a lease with no fixed term, give 3 months' notice) and Section 5, Item 9 (restoration to original condition and the deposit).
- Tax: file your income tax before departure, or designate a tax agent (納税管理人). See Item 3 of this section (income tax when you leave partway through the year). For resident tax, see Item 4 of this section (resident tax for the year you leave and the year after).
- Go to the municipal office to file the moving-out notification. See Item 1 of this section (file the moving-out notification before you leave).
- Ask for several extra originals of your degree certificate and transcript; keep scans of the visa pages of your passport and both sides of your residence card. You will need them for the credential evaluation. See Item 6 of this section (get your degree evaluated after returning).

**On the day of departure**

- If you do not plan to come back, hand in your residence card at the airport. See Item 2 of this section (your residence card becomes invalid at departure).
- For the limit on carrying cash, see Section 4, Item 5 (carrying foreign currency cash into and out of China).

**After returning to China**

- Apply for the lump-sum withdrawal payment within 2 years, if you have decided to take it. See Section 3, Item 4 (claim the lump-sum withdrawal payment within 2 years).
- Get your degree evaluated. See Item 6 of this section (get your degree evaluated after returning).
- Work out whether you count as a resident individual in China for that year, and whether the income from those months in Japan must be declared. See Section 4, Item 1 (tax residence in China) and Section 4, Item 2 (declaring Japanese income to China).
- Connect to the pension insurance in China. See Section 4, Item 8 (pension insurance in China counts cumulative years).
- Driving licence. See Section 4, Item 9 (licence extension and replacement).

### 1. Before you leave, go to the municipal office and file the moving-out notification (転出届), stating the date you leave Japan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One trip to the municipal office (役所).
- In plain words: A person moving out of Japan must notify the municipality in advance, stating where they are going and on what day. Not notifying carries a non-criminal fine of up to 50,000 yen. More important, as long as your resident record (住民票) remains, National Pension, National Health Insurance and resident tax keep being charged to you.
- Benefit: Article 24 of the Basic Resident Register Act (住民基本台帳法): 「転出をする者は、あらかじめ、その氏名、転出先及び転出の予定年月日を市町村長に届け出なければならない。」 ("A person who moves out must notify the mayor of the municipality in advance of their name, the destination and the planned date of moving out.") Article 52, paragraph 2: a person who does not make this notification without just cause 「五万円以下の過料に処する」 ("is subject to a non-criminal fine of up to 50,000 yen"). Article 9, item 2 of the National Pension Act (国民年金法) provides that an insured person loses that status on the day after 「日本国内に住所を有しなくなつたとき」 ("the person ceases to have an address in Japan"). Article 294, paragraph 2 of the Local Tax Act (地方税法) provides that, for municipal resident tax, an individual with an address in a municipality means a person recorded in that municipality's basic resident register.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 住民基本台帳法（昭和四十二年法律第八十一号）第二十四条、第五十二条第二項. e-Gov 法令検索 <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/342AC0000000081>；国民年金法 第九条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/334AC0000000141>；地方税法 第二百九十四条第二項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/325AC0000000226>
- Notes: "As long as the resident record remains, premiums and tax keep being charged" is the result of reading the three provisions above together, not the text of any one of them. How many days ahead you can file at the earliest and what to bring differ by municipality; this section did not verify this. How National Health Insurance status follows your address is in a provision this section did not read. A person who is only going back to China for a short time and will return to Japan does not need to file the moving-out notification. For that case, see Section 1, Item 8 (before leaving Japan for more than 1 year).

### 2. If you do not plan to come back, your residence card becomes invalid on the spot at departure and must be handed in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain words: For a mid- to long-term resident with no re-entry permit, the residence card becomes invalid once the departure confirmation at the airport is done, and must be handed in at once. If you leave on a re-entry permit and do not return before it expires, the card becomes invalid at that expiry and must be handed in within 14 days.
- Benefit: Article 19-14, item 3 of the Immigration Control Act (入管法) provides that when a mid- to long-term resident (中長期在留者) who has not obtained a re-entry permit (再入国許可) under Article 26, paragraph 1 receives departure confirmation from an immigration inspector at the port of departure, the residence card (在留カード) becomes invalid. Item 4 provides that when a person who obtained a re-entry permit has left and does not re-enter within the permit's period of validity, the residence card becomes invalid. Article 19-15, paragraph 2 provides that a card invalidated under item 3 must be returned 「直ちに」 ("immediately"); paragraph 1 provides that a card invalidated under item 4 and certain other items must be returned 「十四日以内」 ("within fourteen days") from the day the cause arose.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第十九条の十四、第十九条の十五. e-Gov 法令検索 <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: A person who states at departure that they will come back is deemed under Article 26-2 to have obtained a re-entry permit; see Section 1, Item 8 (get a re-entry permit before leaving for more than 1 year). If such a person ends up not returning, whether the card becomes invalid under item 4 when the 1 year runs out, and how to hand it in from abroad, was not verified against any explanation from the immigration authorities in this section. If you have already decided not to come back, saying so clearly at the airport and handing in the card is the least trouble. Once the card is handed in, your status of residence is gone, and coming to Japan again means applying anew. So if you are unsure, do not hand it in yet.

### 3. If you leave Japan partway through the year, finish filing your income tax before departure, or first designate a tax agent (納税管理人)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One trip to the tax office before departure, or find someone in Japan to be your tax agent.
- In plain words: A person who needs to file a final tax return (確定申告) and leaves partway through the year must, before departure, file for the income from 1 January of that year to the day of departure. "Departure" here means leaving Japan without having designated a tax agent. If you have designated a tax agent, that person can file for you in the normal filing period. A person who is due a refund may also file at departure.
- Benefit: Article 127, paragraph 1 of the Income Tax Act (所得税法) provides that where a resident departs partway through the year and the income from 1 January of that year to the time of departure is such that a final return must be filed, the return must be filed with the head of the tax office 「その出国の時までに」 ("by the time of that departure"). Paragraph 2 provides that where the case is one in which a refund return may be filed, it may be filed. Article 2, paragraph 1, item 42 defines "departure" (出国) as a resident ceasing to have an address and residence in Japan without having filed the notification of a tax agent under Article 117, paragraph 2 of the Act on General Rules for National Taxes (国税通則法). Article 117 of the Act on General Rules for National Taxes provides that when an individual taxpayer ceases to have an address and residence in Japan, the taxpayer must appoint a tax agent to handle tax returns and other matters, and notify the head of the tax office.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 所得税法 第百二十七条、第二条第一項第四十二号. e-Gov 法令検索 <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/340AC0000000033>；国税通則法 第百十七条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/337AC0000000066>
- Notes: A person with only one salary whose company has already settled the tax on leaving the job does not necessarily need to file a final return in the first place; see Section 3, Item 9 (who does not need to file a tax return themselves). Whether the company will do a settlement equivalent to the year-end adjustment when you leave partway through the year was not verified in this section. Who can be a tax agent and how to write the notification form were not verified against the National Tax Agency's procedure page in this section. If you find out only after leaving that you are due a refund, you can still claim it within 5 years (see Section 3, Item 9 (overpaid tax can be claimed back within 5 years)), but doing it from abroad depends on a tax agent.

### 4. Arrange who will pay your resident tax for the year you leave and the year after
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The resident tax itself. The amount depends on the previous year's income; this section did not verify the calculation.
- In plain words: Resident tax (住民税) is decided by where you live on 1 January. A person who leaves after 1 January still has to pay resident tax for that fiscal year. Since you are no longer in that municipality, you must designate a tax agent to handle it for you, or be approved by the municipality as not needing one.
- Benefit: Article 39 of the Local Tax Act provides that the assessment date (賦課期日) for individual prefectural resident tax is 1 January of the year in which the first day of the fiscal year falls. Article 300, paragraph 1 provides that where a person liable for municipal resident tax has no address, residence or the like in the municipality where the tax is owed, the person must appoint a tax agent to handle all matters concerning payment and report this to the mayor. Paragraph 2 provides that where the person applies to the mayor and is approved on the ground that there is no obstacle to securing collection of that person's municipal resident tax, no tax agent needs to be appointed.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 地方税法 第三十九条、第三百条. e-Gov 法令検索 <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/325AC0000000226>
- Notes: Whether you can pay the remaining resident tax in one lump before leaving, and whether the company will deduct it all from your last pay when you leave the job, are practices of each municipality and company. This section did not verify them; ask the tax division of the municipal office before you leave. If you have the choice, moving out before the end of December rather than in early January makes a difference of a whole year's resident tax. This is an inference from the assessment date, not the text of the provision. If you leave without paying in full, the unpaid tax stays on record and may affect a later application for a status of residence in Japan; see Section 1, Item 10 (what is looked at for permanent residence).

### 5. National Pension status ends the day after you leave Japan, and the 2 years for the lump-sum withdrawal payment count from there
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money.
- In plain words: Once you have no address in Japan, from the next day you are no longer an insured person under the National Pension and no longer pay premiums. The lump-sum withdrawal payment must be applied for within 2 years after that. Decide whether to take it before you leave, because once you take it, those years are reset to zero.
- Benefit: Article 9 of the National Pension Act provides that an insured person loses that status on the day after the day one of the items applies; item 2 is 「日本国内に住所を有しなくなつたとき」 ("when the person ceases to have an address in Japan"). One of the conditions on the Japan Pension Service's page on the lump-sum withdrawal payment is that no more than 2 years have passed since the day the person last lost insured status; where the person had an address in Japan when losing that status, the period counts from the first day afterwards on which the person has no address in Japan.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国民年金法 第九条. e-Gov 法令検索 <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/334AC0000000141>；日本年金機構. 脱退一時金の制度. <https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/jukyu/seido/sonota-kyufu/dattai-ichiji/20150406.html>
- Notes: For the amount, the 60-month cap and "taking it resets the years to zero", see Section 3, Item 4 (claim the lump-sum withdrawal payment within 2 years). A person who has already reached 10 years cannot take the lump sum and can receive a pension in the future; how to receive it abroad was not verified in this section. A person working at a company pays into Employees' Pension Insurance (厚生年金), where status follows the employment relationship and ends on the day of leaving the job; this section did not read that provision. If you may come back to work in Japan in the future, count the later years in when deciding whether to take it.

### 6. After returning, get your degree evaluated: it is done online, costs 360 yuan, and schools in Japan fall under "within 20 working days"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: The evaluation fee is 360 yuan per copy. Allow more than a month for preparing materials and waiting for the result.
- In plain words: Overseas degrees and diplomas must be evaluated by the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange under the Ministry of Education; this is the CSCSE credential evaluation (留服认证). It is done entirely online, with no visit in person. Schools in Japan fall under 「其他国家和地区」 ("other countries and regions"), where the working time limit is within 20 working days; it takes longer if the school is slow to reply or holidays intervene. The materials you submit include the passport and visas from your period of study, an ID photo and an authorization statement. Postdoctoral research certificates are outside the scope of evaluation.
- Benefit: The CSCSE evaluation guide states that the process requires real-person verification in the 「移民局12367」 ("National Immigration Administration 12367") mobile app, to obtain an 8-digit personal authorization code that authorizes the CSCSE to obtain your exit and entry records; you then upload electronic copies of the materials and pay online. The application materials include: the travel documents used during the period of study (passport or travel permit, visas and endorsements); a recent standard ID photo; an authorization statement; and it is recommended to provide supporting materials such as the complete official transcript issued by the institution. The working time limit is managed by category: within 10 working days for most institutions in the United States and Canada and most Chinese-foreign cooperative programmes; within 15 working days for most institutions in the United Kingdom, France, Italy, Australia, New Zealand and Singapore; within 20 working days for 「其他国家/地区」 ("other countries/regions"). The guide notes that the period may be extended in cases such as the awarding institution or the verifying body not sending back the verification result in time. The evaluation fee is 「360元/份」 ("360 yuan per copy"). Where false materials are provided, a notice of non-evaluation is issued and the fee is not refunded. A person who disagrees with the result may apply for a review within 12 months from the date the result was issued, and may apply only once. Certificates outside the scope of acceptance include: diplomas and certificates not at the level of higher education; completion or graduation certificates for foreign language training or other non-degree courses; 「进修人员、访问学者的研究经历证明和博士后研究证明」 ("certificates of research experience for advanced-study trainees and visiting scholars, and postdoctoral research certificates").
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 教育部留学服务中心 (2025-04-02 发布). 国（境）外学历学位认证指南. <https://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/zwfw/lxfwzxwsfwdt2020/xlxwrz32/qtxx/2026042823585825550/index.html>
- Notes: The first item of the materials list (degree certificate and so on) was not read in full in the retrieved page text; go by the original guide page and the "申请材料" (Application Materials) page it links to. The uses of the evaluation (it must be passed first for civil service exams, household registration, professional title review and so on) and the list of institutions under "加强审查" ("enhanced review") are what Section 32, Items 1, 9 and 10 of the original book say; this section did not verify them again. Exit and entry records are pulled by the system, so your exits and entries while abroad need to match your study history. For a person who did a doctorate in Japan over a long period and rarely went back to China, this is not a problem; online courses and degrees obtained without leaving China are a different matter. Postdoctoral experience cannot be evaluated; when looking for a job in China, you prove it with documents such as an employment contract or a certificate of employment. For using documents issued in Japan back in China, see Section 6, Item 2 (a Japanese apostille).
